iOS · iPad + iPhone · for families

One tap. Full screen. Your family is there.

Hebra is a video calling app stripped to the absolute minimum — designed so senior parents never get lost in menus, contacts, or settings.

How it works

1

Pair once

One device becomes the Follower (iPad at home with the parent). The other becomes the Leader (your iPhone). Enter a 6-digit linking code to pair them — done in 30 seconds.

2

Press call

On the Leader phone, tap one giant green button. The Follower iPad rings instantly — even if the app is closed or the screen is locked — via Apple's native CallKit & PushKit integration.

3

Talk face to face

Full-screen, edge-to-edge video fills the iPad. No floating toolbars, no distracting UI — just your face, as large as possible. Hang up when you're done.

Built for the people who need it most

📞

Rings like a real phone call

PushKit wakes the app from any state. CallKit shows the native green call screen. No missed calls because "the app wasn't open."

🔒

Private & encrypted

Direct WebRTC peer-to-peer connection. Video and audio travel encrypted between devices — never through our servers. Zero tracking, zero ads.

🔗

No accounts, no contacts

No email, no phone number, no sign-up. A simple 6-digit code pairs two devices. That's the entire setup.

📱

Locks to one purpose

After setup, the Follower device locks into call-answering mode. No menus to get lost in. The parent's only job is to accept or ignore the call.

Tech under the hood

100% native Swift + SwiftUI on the iOS side. Cloudflare Durable Objects handle signaling over WebSocket with hibernation — keeping costs at zero even under the free tier. ICE/TURN relay via Cloudflare Calls. PushKit VoIP pushes are delivered through Apple APNs with JWT-signed tokens.

The entire backend fits in a single Cloudflare Worker. No database, no storage, no third-party services. The signaling server is stateless between calls.
