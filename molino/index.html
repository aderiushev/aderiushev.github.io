iOS · for Russian speakers learning Spanish

Read Don Quixote in Spanish. Understand every line.

The Russian meaning arrives first, so comprehension is free — and all your attention lands on the Spanish itself.

A passage returns three times

1

Russian first

Each phrase comes in Russian; a tap reveals the Spanish original. An unclear word? Touch it — a small Russian hint appears right above the line.

2

Days later — Spanish first

The same passage comes back with the languages swapped: Spanish leads, Russian waits one tap away in the margin.

3

Almost clean Spanish

By the third return you are reading Cervantes nearly unaided. Finished passages settle on your Shelf; the phrases that still needed hints keep returning as short encounters — always as living lines from the book.

The library starts with the opening of Don Quixote, Part I — and it is growing.

Nothing leaves your phone

Molino is local-first by design. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no network calls — the app cannot phone home because there is nothing in it that does. Everything you read stays in a database on your device. The whole privacy policy fits on one page.
