What is Molino?

A reading app for Russian speakers learning Spanish. You read real literature — starting with Don Quixote — with the Russian meaning arriving first, so the Spanish original can be read with full comprehension. It works completely offline.

I finished a passage and now it's gone. Is that a bug?

That's the heart of the app. A finished passage returns a few days later with more Spanish and fewer hints — the app tells you the date on the completion screen and on the Today screen. Reading it again too soon would spoil the effect, so Molino waits.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for, nothing to log in to, and no password to forget. The app makes no network connections at all.

Where is my reading data?

In a local database on your device — nowhere else. Deleting the app deletes the data. See the privacy policy for the one-page version of this answer.

Will there be more books?

The library is growing passage by passage, starting with Don Quixote, Part I. More chapters — and eventually more books — are being prepared.
