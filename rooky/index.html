iPhone · for preschoolers · not a word on screen

Rooky teaches how the pieces move — not how to win.

No reading, no listening to instructions, no losing. A child learns the knight's jump and the rook's slide the same way they learn everything at four: by watching it happen, hearing it, and feeling it in their hand.

How it teaches, without saying a word

1

Legal squares light up

Touch a piece and every square it can reach glows. Nothing to read, nothing to guess — the board simply shows the shape of the move.

2

One square, one note, one little buzz

The piece slides a square at a time. Each step plays a single gentle tone and a soft haptic tap, so counting is felt long before it can be said out loud.

3

Bumping, not battling

Landing on another piece wakes it up — it startles, giggles, and bursts into stars. A wrong tap just makes a piece bump gently and spring back.

No timers No lives No fail state

Nothing about your child leaves the phone

Rooky collects no data of any kind. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, no in-app purchases — and no network access at all, so there is nothing in the app with a way to send information anywhere. Read the full privacy policy.
